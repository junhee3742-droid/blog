Hi! Vibe Lab Notes now has a mascot. Meet **Neuroni**.

![Happy](/stickers/happy.svg)

As befits a neuroscience blog, Neuroni is modeled on a **neuron**, the nerve cell our brains are built from. You will see Neuroni being happy, puzzled and surprised alongside the posts here.

## Neuroni is built like a real neuron

It is a cute version, but every part maps to real neuron anatomy.

| Neuroni | In a real neuron |
|---|---|
| Round body | **Soma**: the cell body, home of the nucleus |
| Two forked horns on top | **Dendrites**: antennas that receive signals from other neurons |
| Arms | Also **dendrites**, and the key to Neuroni's expressions |
| Curled tail | **Axon**: the long cable that sends signals onward |
| Teal segments on the tail | **Myelin**: insulation that makes signals travel faster |
| Round tip of the tail | **Axon terminal**: where the signal is handed to the next cell |

So Neuroni picks up news with its horns and passes it on to you with its tail, which makes it a fitting mascot for a science blog.

## Design notes

- **Colors:** dark navy outline, pale blue body, teal and yellow accents, matching the blog's lab-notebook palette.
- **White border:** a thick sticker-style border keeps Neuroni visible in dark mode too.
- **Original:** drawn from scratch, not based on any existing character.
