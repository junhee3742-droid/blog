Hi, and welcome to **Vibe Lab Notes**.

I am preparing for a PhD in neuroscience. I am especially interested in how aging leads to neurodegenerative diseases such as Parkinson's disease.

Research comes with an endless stream of papers to find, read and organize. So lately I have been building small tools to help with that, using **vibe coding**: describing what I want to an AI in plain language and letting it write the code.

This blog is where I record that process.

## What I write about

- **Papers**: paper summaries and research trends
- **Projects**: a paper collector, blog-writing automation workflows, apps and other things built with AI

## This blog is a project too

The site itself is my first project built with AI. I will write up the whole process, from choosing a blog platform to building the site and putting it online.

## Korean first, English later

I am writing in Korean first and plan to add English posts over time, so the site was built for both languages from day one. Use the **한국어** button at the top right to switch to the Korean pages.

Thanks for stopping by!
